🏗️ Construction Management

Complete Construction ERP for Odoo 19 — BOQ, Job Costing, RA Billing & More

Developed by Ibrahim Elmasry — Senior Odoo Developer & Implementation Consultant

✨ Key Features

📁 Projects & Sub-Projects

Manage construction projects with site location, permits, images, documents, and insurance details. Break projects into sub-projects with full lifecycle tracking.

📋 Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Create BOQs with dimensions (L×W×H×Nos), auto quantity calculation, product-wise cost breakdown, and work type classification.

📊 Rate Analysis

Detailed rate analysis per unit with material, equipment, labour, overhead, and other cost components.

💰 Budget Management

Plan budgets per work type with planned vs. actual amounts, variance analysis, and progress tracking.

🗂️ Phases / WBS

Hierarchical Work Breakdown Structure with parent-child phases, cost entries, and automatic progress computation.

🛠️ Work Orders

Issue work orders with material, equipment, labour, and overhead lines — full job costing per order.

📦 Material Requisition

Approval workflow (Draft → Under Approval → Approved), one-click Purchase Order creation, and internal transfers from warehouse.

👷 Subcontracting

Subcontract management with consume orders, RA billing cycles, contract vs. billed amounts, and completion certificates.

🧾 Progress Billing (RA Bills)

Raise client invoices from work orders with tax support, invoice creation, and cumulative billing tracking.

✅ Quality Checks

Inspection checklists with pass/fail results, corrective actions, recheck dates, photos, and document attachments.

📝 Tasks & Timesheets

Assign tasks to engineers with priority, deadlines, progress %, Kanban view, and employee timesheets.

💸 Extra Expenses

Track unexpected site expenses with approval workflow by managers.

📈 Real-Time Dashboard

Advanced OWL dashboard with live KPIs: projects, sub-projects, material requisitions, phases, work orders, budgets — all clickable to drill down into filtered views.

🔄 Workflow

Draft → Confirm → Approved → In Progress → Done

Every document follows a clean state workflow with manager-level approval gates on critical actions (Approve Material Requisition, Approve Extra Expense).
